01 / THE PEOPLE BEHIND AURA

GOOD TEACHERS CHANGE THE WAY YOU SEE YOURSELF.

AURA was built around a simple belief: the right teacher does more than guide a class. They create an environment where you feel safe enough to explore, strong enough to challenge yourself and present enough to listen.

Our instructors bring different practices, personalities and perspectives to the studio — but they share one intention: to make every practice feel personal.

04 / HOW WE TEACH

THE MAT IS NOT A STAGE.

We do not teach you how to look like a yoga practitioner. We teach you how to pay attention to the person already standing on the mat.

01

LISTEN

Before changing anything, learn to notice. Your breath, your tension, your energy and the signals your body is already giving you.

02

EXPLORE

Every body moves differently. Our teachers offer options instead of forcing one definition of what a pose should look like.

03

CONNECT

The deeper practice begins when the movement becomes a way to understand yourself beyond the studio.
