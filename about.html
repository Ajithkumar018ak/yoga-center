OUR STORY

A PLACE TO RETURN TO YOURSELF.

AURA is a contemporary yoga space created for people who want to slow down, breathe deeper and reconnect with the part of themselves that gets lost in the noise of everyday life.

EST. 2018 CHENNAI · INDIA
01 / PHILOSOPHY

YOGA IS NOT A PERFORMANCE.

It is a conversation between your breath, your body and the quiet space within.

01

FIND YOUR RHYTHM

01

WE BELIEVE IN CONSISTENCY OVER PERFECTION.

You do not need to touch your toes, stand on your head or arrive with years of experience to belong here.

At AURA, yoga begins exactly where you are. Some days your practice may feel powerful. Some days it may feel quiet. Both are valuable.

OUR BELIEF Small practices create meaningful changes.

02 / THE AURA WAY

DESIGNED FOR REAL LIFE.

We created AURA around a simple idea: wellness should not feel like another responsibility on your calendar.

01

BREATHE

Learn to use the breath as an anchor. Through intentional breathing practices, you create space between the moment and your reaction.

02

MOVE

Build strength, mobility and body awareness through thoughtful movement designed to support the way you actually live.

03

BECOME

The practice does not end when you leave the mat. Carry the awareness, calm and confidence into everything that follows.

04 / OUR JOURNEY

FROM ONE MAT.
TO A COMMUNITY.

What began as a small room and a handful of students has grown into a community of people choosing to make space for themselves.

2018
THE BEGINNING

ONE ROOM. ONE INTENTION.

AURA began with a simple studio, a few mats and a belief that yoga could become a meaningful part of modern life.

2020
GOING DEEPER

PRACTICE BEYOND THE STUDIO.

Our community expanded into guided breathwork, meditation and workshops focused on building sustainable everyday rituals.

2023
THE COMMUNITY

MORE PEOPLE. SAME PURPOSE.

AURA welcomed new teachers, new practices and hundreds of people who wanted to move with intention.

2026
TODAY

THE JOURNEY CONTINUES.

Today, AURA remains committed to creating thoughtful spaces where movement becomes awareness and practice becomes a way of living.

05 / OUR COMMUNITY

COME AS YOU ARE.

You do not have to be flexible. You do not have to be experienced. You do not have to know exactly what you need.

Just arrive. We will meet you where you are and help you discover what your practice can become.
